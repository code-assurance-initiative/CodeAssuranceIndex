# Proposal 0019: The comment period is paused until the steering group exists

- **Number:** 0019
- **Filed:** 2026-11-11
- **Proposer:** Jimmy Borch, for the CAI team.
- **Declared interests:** the proposer maintains CCA, an engine that produces CAI evidence, and the Assure, Assert and
  Assay products of Canine Development built on it; the proposer is also the acting maintainer, i.e. the decider
  ([decision 0011](../decisions/0011-interim-pragmatic-governance.md)). This changes the procedure under which every
  other proposal is decided, so the conflict is general. It moves no number.
- **Moves scores:** no.
- **Status:** Accepted by [decision 0019](../decisions/0019-the-comment-period-is-paused-until-the-steering-group-exists.md)

## Problem

[GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md) promises a comment period after every interim decision: 30 days for a change that
moves a number, 14 otherwise. There is nobody to comment. The steering group does not exist, no unaffiliated reviewer
has been found, and none is expected soon (summer 2027 at the earliest). A period nobody can use is a promise the page
cannot keep; it also makes every decision record carry a date that means nothing, and it has held decisions back
(proposal 0014 waits on a period to 2026-12-06 that no one will use).

## Change

In GOVERNANCE.md: the comment period, before or after adoption, is **paused until the steering group exists**. A
decision record says so instead of naming a period. What stays:

- every decision is recorded, with the conflict and the reasons;
- an objection that does arrive, from anyone, is answered on the record, and an unresolved one from a commenter not
  affiliated with the proposer reopens the decision;
- the steering group may revisit any interim decision when it exists.

The periods named in earlier decision records (0006, 0008, 0009, 0011, 0012, 0013) and the period proposal 0014 waits
on are paused by the same rule.

## Effect on published scores

None.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the period as written.** It is a ritual with no participant, and it delays decisions for nothing.
- **Shorten it.** The problem is that nobody can comment, not the length.
