# Proposal 0012: The standard names no producer: every producer-specific file, default and promotion leaves CAI

- **Number:** 0012
- **Filed:** 2026-11-06
- **Proposer:** Jimmy Borch, for the CAI team (drafted with Claude Code)
- **Declared interests:** the proposer maintains CCA (formerly Watchdog) and the Assure, Assert and Assay products
  of Canine Development, today the only producer CAI publishes. This proposal REMOVES advantages that producer has
  inside CAI; it adds none.
- **Moves scores:** no. No CAI score, no rubric, no answer key and no published benchmark result changes. Files and
  defaults move; the numbers computed from them do not.
- **Kind:** a correction against GOVERNANCE.md's independence promise.
- **Supersedes:** [decision 0008](../decisions/0008-a-scanner-mapping-covers-every-rule-its-scanner-emits.md) (accepted 2026-11-06; its mapping change never took effect in the benchmark repository).
- **Status:** Accepted by [decision 0012](../decisions/0012-the-standard-names-no-producer.md)

## Problem

Issue: [#41](https://github.com/code-assurance-initiative/CodeAssuranceIndex/issues/41).

CAI must be independent of every product it measures. It is not independent of one. When the CCA engine renamed its own
rule ids on 2026-11-02/03, the benchmark stopped crediting 219 of its 223 own rules, and the only way to fix that was a
change inside CAI (proposal 0008, accepted by decision 0008 but never put into effect). A standard that has to change when one producer changes its product is
coupled to that producer, whatever its governance says. The coupling is in three places.

**1. The benchmark is built from one producer's engine** (`code-assurance-initiative/scanner-benchmark`, from commit
`257f61f`, 2026-10-06):
- `mappings/watchdog.json`, one producer's mapping of its own rule ids to CAI concepts, lives in CAI.
- `mappings/watchdog-build/build.py` reads a checkout of that producer's engine (`WATCHDOG_SOURCE`) and writes not only
  that mapping but **CAI's own `taxonomy.json` and `coverage/matrix.json`**. The neutral vocabulary every scanner is
  measured against is generated by one producer's tool from one producer's product.
- `mappings/watchdog_scores.py` turns that producer's `scorecard.json` into scoring input.
- `docs/CONTRACT.md` cites `mappings/watchdog-build/concepts.py` and `discrim.py` as where a concept's scope is
  defined (29 mentions of the producer), and the harness defaults name it (`cai_bench/cli.py` `--mapping` defaults to
  `mappings/watchdog.json`; `cai_bench/baseline.py`, `coverage/render_matrix.py`, `tools/disputes.py`, the README).

**2. CAI's code defaults to and trusts that producer** (`CodeAssuranceIndex`):
- `src/Cai.Cli/Program.cs`: `--producer` defaults to `cca.canine.dev`.
- `src/Cai.Web/Badges/IssuerCatalog.cs`: the default configured issuer is `cca.canine.dev`, with `watchdog` as an alias.
- `src/Cai.Web/Program.cs`: the producer's product sites are on the cross-origin allowlist.
- The site draws product-specific marks (`producers/README.md`: `watchdog`, `assay`) built into the site.

**3. CAI's site promotes that producer:**
- `src/Cai.Web/Components/Layout/MainLayout.razor`: the main call to action "Get a survey →" links to `assure.canine.dev`.
- The glossary (`src/Cai.Web/CaiGlossary.cs`), the llms text (`src/Cai.Web/Program.cs`) and the API reference
  (`src/Cai.Web/Components/Pages/ApiReference.razor`) use that producer's products as the example.

What stays: naming Canine Development as the steward that runs CAI today (a disclosure the independence rules require,
in the footer and in GOVERNANCE.md), the producer listing as data (`producers/producers.json`), published results
attributed to the producer that made them, and history (CHANGELOG, decisions, proposals, ADRs).

## Change

**Principle, added to GOVERNANCE.md:**

> CAI names no producer except as data: an entry in the producer listing, the attribution of a published result, and the
> steward's disclosure. No CAI file is generated from, defaults to, trusts, promotes or is maintained for one producer's
> product, and no change to a producer's product requires a change to CAI.

**1. The benchmark.**
- `taxonomy.json` becomes CAI's own source, edited only through proposals and decisions. Its concept scopes (today in the
  producer's `concepts.py`: `GROUP_SCOPE`, `FILE_SCOPE`, `RESOURCE_SCOPE` and others) become fields of the taxonomy
  entries themselves. `coverage/matrix.json` is generated from the taxonomy and the published mappings a producer chooses
  to submit, by CAI's own tool.
- A producer's mapping and score adapter live with the producer, who maintains them. The harness takes them as input
  (`cai_bench score --mapping <file> --scores <file>`, already supported); `--mapping` has no default. A published result
  names the mapping it was scored with (URL and sha256) so anyone can reproduce it. `mappings/watchdog.json`,
  `mappings/watchdog-build/` and `mappings/watchdog_scores.py` are removed. The mappings CAI itself maintains for its
  reference scorer and for third-party baselines (`cai-reference`, `semgrep`) stay: CAI runs those tools itself.
- `docs/CONTRACT.md` defines every scope in its own words and cites no producer file; the README, the harness, the
  dispute tool and the tests use a neutral example scanner.
- The coverage rule proposal 0008 wanted moves to where it belongs: the harness reports, per scored run, the rule ids of
  results that reach no concept, and a published result lists them (contract 1.8). That makes a producer's blind mapping
  visible without CAI ever holding the mapping.

**2. Defaults and trust.** `Cai.Cli` has no default producer. The issuer catalogue has no built-in issuer: issuers come
from configuration and the producer listing. The cross-origin allowlist names no producer site. Marks are drawn from the
producer listing's data, not built into the site.

**3. Promotion.** The site's call to action leads to the producer listing ("Producers that issue surveys"), which shows
every listed producer alike. The glossary, the llms text and the API reference use neutral examples.

## Effect on published scores

None. No CAI score, rubric, key or benchmark result moves. The producer whose mapping leaves CAI keeps scoring with the
same mapping content from its own repository; re-scored on its latest arm, the numbers are identical.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the mapping in CAI and fix it by proposal each time** (proposal 0008). Rejected: CAI would change whenever one
  producer changed its product, which is the coupling itself.
- **Rename the files neutrally but keep them in CAI.** Rejected: a file maintained for one producer is that producer's
  file whatever it is called.

## Questions for the decider

1. Should the steward disclosure stay in the site footer, or move to the About and Governance pages only?
2. Should the published results under `results/watchdog/` move to the producer's product name (decision 0007), or stay
   under the name they were published with?
