# Decision 0019: The comment period is paused until the steering group exists

- **Decision:** 0019
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decided by:** Jimmy Borch, for the CAI team (the acting maintainer, under the interim pragmatic rule of
  [decision 0011](0011-interim-pragmatic-governance.md))
- **Proposal:** [0019: The comment period is paused until the steering group exists](../proposals/0019-the-comment-period-is-paused-until-the-steering-group-exists.md)
- **Outcome:** Accepted as written.
- **Supersedes:** the comment-period clause of decision 0011 (point 2 of its rule), for as long as the steering group
  does not exist.

## Conflicts and recusals

The proposer and the decider are the same person, who also maintains the CCA engine measured by the benchmark. This
decision changes the procedure under which that person decides every other proposal. Nobody else can decide today, so
nobody recused. The safeguards that remain do not depend on a comment period: every decision is recorded with its
reasons, is reversible, can be reopened by an unaffiliated objection whenever one arrives, and is open to the steering
group's review.

## Comment

None, and by this decision none is held.

## Reasons

- There is nobody to comment: no steering group and no unaffiliated reviewer, and none expected before summer 2027 at
  the earliest.
- A promise the page cannot keep is worse than none: it makes decision records carry dates that mean nothing, and it
  held decisions back for nothing.

## What took effect

- Proposal 0019's status, and the proposal and decision indexes.
- [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md): point 2 of the interim rule, step 3 and step 4 of the decision process and the
  note under bar 2 now say that the comment period is paused until the steering group exists.
- The periods named in decisions 0006, 0008, 0009, 0011, 0012 and 0013, and the one proposal 0014 waits on, are paused
  by the same rule. Those records are not edited.
